# The testing round — next 4 days

**Each team's app is tested by ~4 other teams.** The ring: you test the two teams ahead of you and
the two behind you (Team 3 tests 1, 2, 4, 5 — wrap around the ends). Nobody tests their own.

## Instrument before you leave (owner team)

- **PostHog**: pageviews + **one key event** that maps to a North Star input from your Week-2 Brief.
- **Feedback form** (Tally/Typeform), linked from the app — exactly three questions:
  1. What were you trying to do?
  2. Where did it fight you?
  3. Would you come back? Why / why not?

## Testing (as a tester)

- Use it **for real** for 10 minutes — your actual task, not a courtesy click-through.
- Don't file bugs in person; put everything in the form. Blunt is kind.
- Do all four of your assigned apps **by Friday**.

## The rerun (owner team, by next class)

Read what came back — analytics *and* forms — honestly. Then **log documented experiment #2** in the
spike-log format: what we asked · what we saw · what we changed (or refused to change, and why).
Bring the rerun to Week 6; the vertical slice builds on the survivor.
