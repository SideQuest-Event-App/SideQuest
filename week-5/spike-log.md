# Spike log — one page per spike

> A spike that **kills** your favourite bet is a **successful** spike. "Killed, documented, and
> here's why" is a full-marks result.

- **The bet:** what we believe, in one sentence
- **The dangerous question:** the riskiest assumption underneath it
- **Kill criterion (decided *before* running):** if ___ happens, we drop this
- **What we did:** the probe, in 2–3 lines (timebox: ___ min)
- **What we saw:** honestly, including the failures
- **Decision:** kept / killed / needs another spike
- **What it cost:** time + API spend
