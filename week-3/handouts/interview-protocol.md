# Discovery interview protocol — Week 3

**The point:** mine for a real story with friction in it. You are *not* validating your idea.

## Pairing (the ring)

Each team interviews members of the **next team over** — Team 1 ↔ Team 2's members, Team 3 ↔ Team 4's,
and so on around the ring. **Nobody interviews their own team** — you know your own product too well to
hear anything new. Aim for **3–4 recorded interviews per team** across two rounds.

## Setup (1 minute)

- **10 minutes each way**, then swap.
- **Record it** — anything that gives you a transcript: Meet, Teams, Zoom, Otter, a phone's voice memo.
- **Consent, out loud, every time:** *"I'm recording this for a class exercise — is that okay?"*

## Ask stories, not opinions

Start from a real, recent moment in your team's problem space and dig:

- *"Tell me about the **last time** you …"*
- *"Walk me through what you actually did, step by step."*
- *"What was the hardest / most annoying part of that?"*
- *"What did you do about it? What did you try before that?"*
- *"When did you give up / work around it?"*

Follow the sigh. When they hesitate, laugh, or apologize — that's the data. Ask *"what do you mean?"*
and wait.

## Never ask

- ❌ *"Would you use an app that …?"* (everyone says yes; it means nothing)
- ❌ *"Don't you think it would be better if …?"* (that's your pitch wearing a question)
- ❌ Anything about **your solution**. Tonight is about **their life**.

## Afterward

Save the transcript where your team can reach it — it feeds the synthesis worksheet next.
