# Synthesis worksheet — from transcripts to tree to build

## 1 · Pull opportunities (with receipts)

Paste each transcript into your AI and ask, verbatim:

> List the opportunities — needs, pains, desires — in this interview.
> **Quote the exact words** for each one.
> **Flag anything you inferred but can't quote.**

**The trace rule: no quote, no branch.** If the model (or you) can't point at the sentence, it's an
**assumption** — keep it, but mark it clearly (grey it out / tag it `[assumption]`). Assumptions are
things to test, not evidence.

*Thin on transcripts? Don't let the model roleplay your user — a synthetic user will flatter you.
Whatever it invents goes in as `[assumption]`.*

*Several transcripts? NotebookLM (free) is good at synthesizing across them — optional.*

## 2 · Build the tree

```
OUTCOME  (root)      = your North Star, from last week's Brief
├── OPPORTUNITY      = a need/pain/desire, in their words   ← each carries its quote
│   ├── solution idea
│   └── solution idea
├── OPPORTUNITY  [assumption — no quote yet]
│   └── solution idea
└── ...
```

- Opportunities are **their** problem, phrased from their life — not your feature in disguise.
- A crummy first tree from 3 interviews is **correct** tonight. The tree is never finished.

## 3 · Wire ONE path

Pick the branch with the strongest quote behind it. Change **one thing** in Sketch 0 so the build
answers it. One is the discipline — you're starting the loop, not finishing it.

**Leaving tonight:** transcripts saved · a first tree with quotes on it · one wired change ·
and (logbook) *what did you hear that you did not want to hear?*
