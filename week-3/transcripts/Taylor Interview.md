# Informal in class Interview 2
## Key points and Tree
**Topic:** User research interview about event attendance habits (film festival appearance by a favourite actress)

**Key points:**

- **Most recent event:** A Toronto film festival (likely TIFF), attended to see an actress they're a fan of, who was there to promote her film
- **Primary motivation:** Seeing a specific person (their "idol") in person; the event itself mattered less than who would be there ("maybe the core thing is the person")
- **Discovery method:** Actively searched across many platforms ("little rap book", likely Xiaohongshu/RedNote, plus Google and other social media) to find the exact location the actress would appear, then arrived early to get a front spot
- **Most memorable aspect:** Simply seeing the person; that one moment was the point of the whole outing
- **Future intent:** Would attend similar events, but only if they know and love the person's work; a person they only vaguely know isn't enough
- **Baseline behaviour:** Rarely goes out by choice (personal preference, and "too cold most of the time"); comfortable staying home, which makes events like this feel rare and "precious"
- **Social pattern:** Lives with someone who also prefers staying home (video games, etc.); doesn't seek out events, but rarely refuses when a friend invites them
- **Decision factors, ranked:**
    1. **Location / transport:** Distance, traffic jams, and multi-leg trips (TTC → train → Uber) are deterrents; around 30 minutes of transit is acceptable
    2. **Price:** Depends on their finances at the time; a $200 ticket is "a big no"

**Overall takeaway:** This person's attendance is driven by *who* will be at an event rather than *what* the event is: they'll go far out of their usual stay-at-home routine for a rare chance to see someone they admire. Attendance is gated by travel effort (≈30 min transit max) and cost, and outside of idol-driven events, friend invitations are the main thing that gets them out of the house.

## Flagged inferences (no direct quote to support them)

- **Inferred pain: Event information is scattered across platforms.** The user searched "everywhere like every platform" to find one location, which suggests the information was hard to find, but they never described this as frustrating. It's inferred from behaviour, not stated.
- **Inferred need: Discovery that follows people, not categories.** Because "the core thing is the person," this user would likely respond better to "your favourite actress is appearing here" than to interest-based matching like "film events near you." This is my reading of their priorities, not something they asked for.
- **Inferred need: A high bar for leaving home.** Since staying home is their comfortable default, an event has to feel rare or special to be worth the effort. This is inferred from the contrast between "I don't really like to go out" and "really precious opportunity."
- **Inferred lever: Friend invitations as a trigger.** They don't seek out events, but "won't refuse the invitation much." This suggests invitations from friends may be a stronger driver than recommendations, but they never said they'd *want* to be invited more.
- **Transcription note:** "little rap book" is most likely Little Red Book (Xiaohongshu/RedNote), which would mean they use Chinese-language social platforms for discovery. This should be confirmed with the participant, not assumed.
