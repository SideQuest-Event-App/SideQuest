# Informal in class Interview
## Key points and Tree
**Topic:** User research interview about event attendance habits (anime/cosplay conventions and similar events)

**Key points:**

- **Most recent event:** Fan Expo (anime/cosplay convention) in August, attended with friends
- **Primary motivation:** Buying merchandise from artists — conventions are seen as a concentrated place to find artists selling their work that aren't available in daily life
- **Secondary motivation:** Socializing — cosplaying together with friends, meeting new people who share interests, and taking photos together
- **Attendance pattern:** Usually goes with existing friends, but open to meeting new people at the event (especially other cosplayers)
- **Decision factors, ranked:**
    1. **Location** — strong preference for downtown/convenient transit access (mentions "just take Union"); events in Waterloo are seen as too far (3-4 hour drive) and are a deterrent
    2. **Price** — cost sensitivity, including choosing to attend on weekdays when ticket prices are cheaper than weekends
- **Most memorable aspects:** Capturing photos of the moment and socializing with friends
- **Future intent:** Plans to keep attending this type of event annually (frames it as a yearly tradition), driven by the same location/price factors
- **Other events attended:** Food festivals — motivated by trying different foods and casual hangout time with friends
- **Discovery method:** Social media (Instagram) — event organizers/pages post about upcoming weekend events
- **Decision to attend:** Largely driven by whether friends are interested in going together, not a solo decision

**Overall takeaway:** This person's event attendance is driven by two main motivators (shopping/merch access and social bonding with friends) and gated primarily by two practical constraints (location/convenience and price), with social media as the main discovery channel and friend group interest as the deciding factor.
